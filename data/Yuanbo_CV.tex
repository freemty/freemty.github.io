%----------RESEARCH OVERVIEW----------
\section{Research Overview}

My research centers on \textbf{vision foundation models}, with a focus on understanding their internal mechanisms and improving their design. Key areas include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.2in]
  \item \textbf{Understanding and Improving:} Uncovering the mechanisms of generative models to guide efficient architecture design.
  \item \textbf{World Simulators:} Developing interactive and realistic vision generative models as world simulators.
  \item \textbf{Grounding with Generative Priors:} Leveraging generative priors for precise grounding in real-world environments.
\end{itemize}
