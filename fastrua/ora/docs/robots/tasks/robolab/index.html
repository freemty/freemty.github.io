For each: the upstream default instruction, the native-action limit (the upstream episode length at 15 Hz) and the public completion contract ORA gives the model. Success is decided only by RoboLab's own success termination; the contract restates it and never replaces it. Generated from ora/environment/benchmarks/robolab.py (TASKS) and ora/policy/environments/robolab/task-contracts/registry-v1.json.

BananaThenRubiksCubeTask

Instruction. put the banana then the cube in the bowl

Native action limit. 900 (60 s at 15 Hz).

Completion contract.

  • Both the banana and the cube must end up inside the bowl, each touching it.
  • The banana must come to rest in the bowl before the cube does: the order is fixed the first time each of them rests there, released, and taking one out again does not undo it.
  • Let go of each of them: the task is not complete while the gripper still touches either one.

BananasInBinOneMoreTask

Native action limit. 900 (60 s at 15 Hz).

Completion contract.

  • Of the bananas that start on the table, exactly one must end up inside the grey bin; a second one inside the bin as well does not complete the task.
  • Let go of it there: a banana the gripper still touches does not count.
  • Leave the bananas that are already in the bin there.
