Mistral: Voxtral TTS, Forge, Leanstral, & what's next for Mistral 4 — w/ Pavan Kumar Reddy & Guillaume Lample — Mar 30 | link | youtube
TLDR: Mistral releases Voxtral TTS, a 3B open-weights TTS model with a novel auto-regressive + flow-matching architecture and in-house neural audio codec. Achieves ElevenLabs-level quality at a fraction of the cost. Guillaume Lample discusses Mistral's strategy of open-weight models enabling enterprise fine-tuning on proprietary data. Chapters: Voxtral TTS Architecture — 3B model based on Ministral, combines auto-regressive semantic token generation with flow-matching for acoustic tokens. Novel in-house neural audio codec converts audio to latent tokens at lower bitrate. Enterprise & Fine-tuning — Key pitch: enterprises have trillions of domain-specific tokens that closed models can't leverage. Open weights let customers fine-tune on proprietary data for massive quality gains. Real-time Voice Agents — Discussion of streaming capabilities for voice agent deployment. Key Quotes: Guillaume Lample: "When customers use off-the-shelf closed models, what's very sad is they are not leveraging data they have been collecting for years or decades... trillions of tokens in their specific domain." Pavan Kumar Reddy: "It's a novel architecture — auto-regressive flow-matching with a new in-house neural audio codec converting audio into semantic and acoustic tokens."
