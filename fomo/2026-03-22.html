Terence Tao — Kepler, Newton, and Mathematical Discovery (Mar 20) | link
Tao compares Kepler to a "high-temperature LLM" — spending twenty years trying random hypotheses, ultimately discovering elliptical orbits through Brahe's precision data. Core thesis: AI has driven hypothesis generation cost to near-zero, but verification is now the bottleneck. Peer review is being overwhelmed by AI submissions. Human-AI hybrids will dominate math longer than expected because judging an idea's future value requires decades of cultural context.
