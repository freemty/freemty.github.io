align_blocks

Native action limit. 200

Completion contract.

  • All three blocks must end up in one straight row, with their centres lying on a single straight line.
  • Each block must also be squared to that row, its sides running along and straight across the line of the row; a block left twisted at an angle to the row does not count, even if its centre is on the line.
  • Every block must stay down on the table the whole time: if any block is ever lifted off the table or raised up onto anything, even briefly, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure, and setting it back down does not undo it.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

arrange_largest_number

Native action limit. 1050

Completion contract.

  • Use all the number pieces to form the largest possible number, read from left to right along world +X: the most significant digit goes on the pad furthest toward world -X, on the side of the table where the left arm is mounted, and each piece must be centred on its own pad.
  • Lay every piece flat so the number reads correctly from the robot's side of the table, with the top of each numeral pointing toward the far edge (world +Y).
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

build_tower

Native action limit. 1050

Completion contract.

  • The four upright blocks start as two pairs, one pair on each side of the table (world -X and world +X). The longest board must rest across one block from each pair, and the second-longest board must rest across the remaining block of each pair, those two blocks standing on the longest board.
  • The second-longest board must sit centred over the middle of the longest board, not merely somewhere on its two blocks.
  • Centre the smallest board above the second-longest board and the roof-shaped piece above that; align their long edges and keep the roof upright.
  • Keep the blocks upright and the boards level.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

cover_blocks

Native action limit. 800

Completion contract.

  • Left to right runs from world -X, the side of the table where the left arm is mounted, toward world +X, the right-arm side. The blocks' left-to-right order is the order in which they stand when the episode resets.
  • A block counts as covered when one of the three cups, upside down with its opening toward the table, has been lowered over it so that the whole block is inside the cup and the cup's rim is down around the block rather than above it. A cup hovering above a block or resting tilted on it does not cover it, and a block with no cup over it in this way is uncovered.
  • The official check looks for six arrangements one after another, each at some moment after the one before: first only the leftmost block covered; then the leftmost and middle blocks covered while the rightmost is still uncovered; then all three covered; then the red block uncovered while the green and blue blocks are still covered; then the red and green blocks uncovered while the blue block is still covered; and last all three uncovered. The task is complete only once this whole sequence has been passed through and has ended with all three blocks uncovered.
  • All three cups must be upside down, with their openings toward the table, at each of those six moments, including the last.
  • The blocks themselves must stay where they are: if any block is ever pushed, knocked or carried away from the place where it stood when the episode reset, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure, and moving it back afterwards does not undo it.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

fold_clothes

Native action limit. 500

Completion contract.

  • Fold each sleeve across the torso toward the opposite side of the chest.
  • Fold the lower body of the garment toward the shoulders, bringing each side of the hem near its corresponding shoulder and aligning the hem edge with the shoulder edge.

general_pickup

Native action limit. 200

Completion contract.

hang_mugs

Native action limit. 800

Completion contract.

  • All three mugs must end up hanging on the mug rack, each one clear of the table and carried by the rack.
  • Each mug must hang by its handle, with a peg of the rack passing through the handle and the body of the mug hanging below it, not merely resting against the rack or set on top of it.
  • Each mug's handle must end up well above the table, up at the height of the rack's pegs, rather than sagging below the peg that holds it.
  • All three mugs must end up threaded along the same one horizontal direction. The rack's pegs run along two directions at right angles to each other: its lowest pair and its highest pair share one direction, and its middle pair runs crosswise to that. Only the direction the lowest and highest pairs share counts, and either way along it counts; a mug threaded along the crosswise direction does not count, however securely it hangs.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

imitate_sorting_sequence

Native action limit. 1600

Completion contract.

  • Your basket is the one on the left-arm side of the table, toward world -X, and the nearer of the two to the robot. The other basket, on the right-arm side toward world +X and farther from the robot, is the one the demonstrating arm fills.
  • Keep both robot arms at their initial poses while the other arm demonstrates the placement sequence.
  • Place the corresponding objects into your basket in the observed order, leaving later objects outside until their turn.
  • Keep the demonstrated objects in the other basket. Do not release and leave a later object in your basket ahead of its predecessors.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

The demonstration is scripted by the environment and advances only while native actions are consumed: it does not progress while you look at images or plan. Wait with both arms held still while it runs, and look again before acting on the order you saw.

Teeth still along the slit, turned anticlockwise, or turned square across the slit or further do not count.

make_kong

Native action limit. 600

Completion contract.

  • The opponent's discard, one of the opponent's standing tiles across the table being knocked over, advances only while robot commands are consuming native actions; while no robot command executes, the opponent does not move.
  • If either robot arm is away from its reset pose while the opponent's arm is away from its own resting pose, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure; moving the arm back afterwards does not undo it.
  • Lay the three tiles in your standing row that match the discarded tile down flat, face up, and keep every other tile in that row standing.
  • The draw tile is the face-down tile on top of the stack nearer the robot at the left end of the table (world -X). The official check first requires the matching tiles to lie face up while the draw tile still lies face down as it started; after that, the draw tile must stand upright in the next place at the open right end of your row (world +X), in line with the row, with both grippers open and the other tiles unchanged.
  • This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.

organize_table

Native action limit. 1000

Completion contract.

  • Place the mouse on its mouse pad, the keyboard lying flat over its marked frame so that it covers the middle of the frame, the figurine upright on its stand, and the alarm clock upright on top of the drawer unit, and let each placed object come to rest.

  • The instruction's final step, opening the drawer and putting remaining items inside, is not part of the official completion criteria.

play_tic_tac_toe

Native action limit. 1100

Completion contract.

  • Your pieces are the five rings; the opponent, the separate robot arm across the table, plays the four crosses. You move first.
  • The opponent takes its turn only when the board shows exactly one more of your rings than of its crosses, each centred in a cell, and both of your arms are back at their reset poses. Its move advances only while robot commands are consuming native actions; while no robot command executes, the opponent does not move.
  • If either of your arms is away from its reset pose while the opponent's arm is away from its own resting pose, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure; moving the arm back afterwards does not undo it.
  • The game is complete only when the board is full: all nine pieces, your five rings and the opponent's four crosses, each lying on the board and centred in a cell of its own. Making three in a row does not end the game, and which side wins is not judged.
  • This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.

play_Xylophone

Native action limit. 500

Completion contract.

  • Left to right runs from world -X, the side of the table where the left arm is mounted, toward world +X, the right-arm side. Every key must be struck, one after another in the order the keys lie along the xylophone, beginning with the key at its left end and ending with the key at its right end.
  • A key counts as struck when the head of the mallet comes down to that key, touching it or just above it, over the key itself rather than between keys or beside the xylophone. Contact by any other part of the mallet, or by a gripper, does not count.
  • After each strike the mallet must be lifted clearly back up from that key before a strike on the next key counts; sliding the mallet along the keys without lifting it counts as a single strike at most.
  • A key counts only once every key to its left has been struck in turn; a key struck out of turn must be struck again later. The task is complete at the moment the key at the right end is struck.
  • This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.

pour_by_language

Native action limit. 800

Completion contract.

  • Each bottle's liquid must go into the bowl the instruction pairs it with, the bottles and bowls being told apart by the colours the instruction names: the first bottle named into the first bowl named, the second into the second and the third into the third. Liquid poured into any other bowl does not count for that bottle.
  • A bottle's pour counts only when just a little of its liquid is left in it and almost none of the liquid that has left it lies anywhere but inside its paired bowl. Small droplets lying scattered apart from the rest of that liquid are disregarded, but if such scattered droplets add up to a sizeable share of it, that pour does not count.
  • Each bottle's pour is judged at the moment that bottle comes back close to upright, neck up, after having been tipped away from upright, whether it is still held or already set down. Its pour must already count at that moment; if it does not, only a later fresh tip and return to upright of that bottle is judged. A bottle left tipped, lying on its side or upside down is never judged. A pour once judged to count stays counted, and the task is complete, and the episode ends, as soon as all three pours have been judged to count; the arm reset named at the end of the instruction is therefore never judged.
  • The instruction lists the pours in order, first to third, and that order is checked whenever an arm returns to its reset pose. If at that moment a bowl named later in the instruction holds enough of its bottle's liquid for that pour to count while a bowl named earlier does not, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure that nothing done afterwards can undo. An earlier bowl whose liquid has since been spilled or knocked out counts as not holding it.
  • This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.

pour_liquid_into_cup

Native action limit. 400

Completion contract.

  • Pour the bottle's liquid into the cup: only a little of it may be left in the bottle, and almost none of the liquid that has left the bottle may lie anywhere but inside the cup.
  • Small droplets lying scattered apart from the rest of the liquid are disregarded, but if such scattered droplets add up to a sizeable share of the liquid, the pour does not count.
  • The pour is judged at the moment the bottle comes back close to upright, neck up, after having been tipped away from upright, whether it is still held or already set down. Everything above must already be true at that moment; if it is not, only a later fresh tip and return to upright is judged. A bottle left tipped, lying on its side or upside down is never judged, however much liquid is in the cup. The task is complete, and the episode ends, at the first such moment at which everything above is true.
  • This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.

press_by_number

Native action limit. 700

Completion contract.

  • Each red button must be pressed exactly as many times as the number shown on the number card that stands behind it, on the far side of the button from the robot.
  • The presses must come in this order: first every press of the red button on the left, toward world -X on the side of the table where the left arm is mounted; then one press of the blue button; then every press of the red button in the middle; then a second press of the blue button. The blue button is pressed twice in all, once after each red button's presses.
  • A press counts once the button has been pushed well down, and it is finished only when the button has been let go and has sprung all the way back up; the next press must start from the fully raised button.
  • Every press of any button is counted, whenever it happens. Pressing a button more often than this sequence calls for ends the episode at once as a permanent failure that cannot be undone, and a press made out of turn still counts, so it leaves the sequence impossible to complete.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

  • The block must be lying flat on the table the same way up as it started.
  • The block must stay down on the table surface throughout the attempt; if it is ever raised or tipped up off the table, the episode ends at once as a permanent failure, and setting the block back down does not undo that.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

solve_equation

For a symbol that looks the same either way round, the right way round is the way it lay when the episode reset; a piece turned sideways or end for end does not count.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

sort_nesting_dolls_by_size

Native action limit. 1050

Completion contract.

  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.

stack_blocks

swap_blocks

Native action limit. 700

Completion contract.

  • Swapping means that each block ends up on the mat where the other block stood when the episode reset. The two blocks look alike, so the finished arrangement looks the same as the start; it counts only if each block has actually been moved to the other's mat.
  • The official check looks for exactly three moves, one after another: first one block is moved from its own mat onto the mat that was empty at the reset; then the other block is moved onto the mat the first block came from; and last the first block is moved from the formerly empty mat onto the mat the second block came from. Either block may go first. A move is finished only when its block has been set down on the middle of its new mat while the other block rests on its mat.
  • A move counts only if its block is lifted clearly up off the mats on the way; a block slid, pushed or carried only just above the mats does not count as moved.
  • After each move is finished, the button must be pressed once: pushed firmly down, then let go so that it springs back up, before the next move begins. The task is complete once the button has sprung back up after the press that follows the third move.
  • The button may be pressed only three times in the whole episode, once after each move. Every press counts toward that limit whenever it is made, including one made before a move is finished or an extra press to be sure, and a fourth press ends the episode at once as a permanent failure; letting go of the button afterwards does not undo it.
  • Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the same instant that everything else in this contract is true.
